Raytracer

Overview

For this project, I built a CPU-based ray tracer from scratch using OpenGL. I implemented a range of rendering techniques, from ray intersections with basic primitives and meshes to more advanced features such as global illumination and Monte Carlo sampling. I also developed a simple denoiser using bilateral filtering to help reduce noise in the final renders.

Key Features

  • Global illumination
  • BRDF materials
  • Monte Carlo sampling
  • Multi-threaded rendering
  • Primitives and Mesh support
  • Bounding Volume Hierarchy ray intersection optimization
  • Depth of Field
  • Bilateral Filtering denoiser

What I Learned

This project gave me a strong understanding of the maths and concepts behind ray tracing. More importantly, it gave me the opportunity to take those concepts beyond the theory and implement them in code, which helped me build confidence in translating mathematical ideas into practical solutions.
