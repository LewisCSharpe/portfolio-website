Deity's Gambit

Best Student Game 2025 - Deity's Gambit - Shortlisted

Overview

This project was a university group assignment where our team of six created a vertical slice demo in Unity.

The game is a multiplayer scavenger horror game where up to 4 players can join a lobby and have to explore dungeons to collect items whilst avoiding enemies. These items are then to be brought back to the casino lobby to feed into a roulette wheel and earn money towards a quota. Players can also gamble their earnings for a chance to win more, with certain numbers triggering different events and gameplay modifiers.

My Contribution

I worked as a general programmer and level designer where I was responsible for player movement, the roulette wheel mechanics and implementing the multiplayer functionality. I was also responsible for designing and modelling the dungeon levels where I worked closely with teammates to bring the environments together using their prop models and textures.

What I Learned

The project gave me the valuable experience of working in a small team on a larger game project, helping me develop my communication, time management and collaborative development skills. I also gained a better understanding of multiplayer game development through implementing Unity Netcode.
